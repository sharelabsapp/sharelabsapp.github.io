Share Labs – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Share Labs is a private desktop application used by a small team to schedule and publish short videos they created to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It runs only on the team's own computers. There is no public service and no other users.

What data the app uses

  • Basic account information returned by the platforms after the account owner signs in (account ID, username, display name, channel name).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok, used only to upload videos the account owner has approved and to read basic statistics (view counts) of those videos.
  • Captions, hashtags and the video files the team uploads.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is stored only locally on the team's computer. Tokens are stored in a private folder that is never uploaded to any server or code repository.
  • Nothing is posted without a human approving each post in the app.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • Data is sent only to the platform where the account owner chose to publish (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok).

Deleting your data

The account owner can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which deletes its token from the computer. Access can also be revoked in the platform's own settings (Google Account permissions, Instagram/Facebook business integrations, TikTok "Manage app permissions").
