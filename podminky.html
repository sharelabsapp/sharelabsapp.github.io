Share Labs – Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Share Labs is a private, non-commercial desktop tool used only by its owners to publish their own short videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs.

  • Only the owners of the connected accounts may use the app. Each account is connected by its owner signing in on the platform's official login page.
  • Every post is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. The app does not like, follow, comment or send messages.
  • Users must follow the terms and community guidelines of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok and must only publish content they own or have the rights to.
  • The app is provided as is, without warranty.
